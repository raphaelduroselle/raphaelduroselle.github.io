I am Research scientist at Agence Ministérielle pour l'Intelligence Artificielle de Défense (AMIAD).

My research focuses on speech processing. I am particularly interested in the robustness of language and speaker recognition systems.

I received my PhD in 2021 from Université de Lorraine, under the supervision of Denis Jouvet and Irina Illina. Before joining AMIAD, I worked at Direction Générale de l'Armement and Inria Defence & Security.


## Internships

We are looking for students to join several research projects. Topics include:

- Simulating radio communication data to improve the robustness of speech processing systems
- Detecting audio deepfakes using a database of signatures
- Investigating the robustness, detection, and identification of audio steganography and watermarking
- Streaming masking of personal information in speech

Contact me if you are interested.
